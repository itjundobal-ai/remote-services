FAQ

Before we start

Can every computer problem be fixed remotely?

No. Hardware damage, some printer problems and physical network issues may require home service.

Do you ask for passwords?

Customers should not send passwords through the booking form. If a login is needed during support, the customer should enter it directly whenever possible.

Can I request something not listed?

Yes. Choose “Other Digital Request” and describe what you need. We will confirm first if we can do it.
